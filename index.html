About

I am a Researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, working at the intersection of power electronics, converter-dominated power systems, and renewable-hydrogen energy systems. My research combines modelling, control, optimization, and experimental validation of converter-interfaced resources, including electrolyzers, renewable generation, battery storage, and multi-port power converters.

I aim to connect converter-level innovation with power-system operation, with particular interests in weak-grid stability, impedance-based analysis, low-ripple and high-efficiency conversion, fault-tolerant control, and coordinated battery–hydrogen flexibility.
